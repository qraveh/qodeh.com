The Atlas maps quantum computing as it is being built: every technology (111) and every quantum machine built, announced or planned (182), analysed, summarised and partitioned by seven stable design attributes, then compared and combined in dozens of ways. Each technology has a brief and each machine a card; every record has a page of its own in every language.

**Status:** edition **2026.09**, published on Zenodo on 30 September 2026 ([10.5281/zenodo.22674815](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22674815)), in English, Russian and Hebrew, under CC BY 4.0. The site carries the corrections made since; the [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/qraveh/qt-map/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) lists them.
